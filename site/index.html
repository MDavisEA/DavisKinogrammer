Video won't open?

If a video fails to open, it is almost always because the browser can't decode it. iPhone videos use HEVC (H.265), which Chrome decodes with your computer's graphics hardware.

  1. Turn on hardware acceleration in Chrome. Open chrome://settings/system, switch on Use graphics acceleration when available, then click Relaunch. You can confirm it worked at chrome://gpu, where Video Decode should say Hardware accelerated.
  2. Try Safari (Mac or iPhone). It opens iPhone video without any setup.
  3. Convert the video to H.264 MP4 with a free tool such as HandBrake and open the new file.
  4. Record in a compatible format next time. On iPhone: Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible.
